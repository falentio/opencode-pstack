### Trace forensics

**Outcome.** Own the diagnosis from the artifact. Load it, shape it, narrow it to the cause, and attribute it to source. Use this playbook for a dropped `.cpuprofile`, `Trace-*.json.gz`, `Spindump.txt`, or `.heapsnapshot` paired with "why is this slow / unresponsive / leaking / crashing".

**Boundary.** Runtime forensics instruments the live process. Trace forensics starts with an existing capture. Treat the capture as a fixed dataset. Read it and do not re-run it. Keep the tooling generic. Use a DevTools or trace parser for `.cpuprofile` and `.json.gz`, a text editor for a spindump, and heap tooling for a heapsnapshot.

1. Identify the format and load it with the right tool. Parse large artifacts in a subagent using the **principle-guard-the-context-window** skill. Keep the reduced finding in the main thread. **Completion check.** The format, tool, and reduced finding for every large artifact are recorded in the main thread.
2. Transform the raw artifact into a form you can query. Dump the trace or heap snapshot into `sqlite`, with one row per sample, frame, or node. Reach the queryable shape before you read. **Completion check.** The raw artifact has a queryable representation, and every trace or heap snapshot has the required `sqlite` rows before causal reading begins.
3. Narrow to the cause. Query for the frames that hold the most time and walk the call tree to the hot path. For a leak, follow the retainer chain from the leaked object to a GC root. For a spindump, find the thread stuck on-CPU or blocked and record its wait reason. **Completion check.** The relevant hot path, retainer chain, or spindump thread and wait reason are recorded as the candidate cause.
4. Attribute to source. Map the hot frame to its file, symbol, and line through the artifact's own symbols. A frame with no source mapping is not yet a diagnosis. Resolve the symbols, or state plainly that the artifact does not carry them. **Completion check.** Every hot frame has a file, symbol, and line, or the finding states that the artifact lacks source mappings after symbol resolution was attempted.
5. Confirm against a paired capture when you have one. Diff the before and after artifacts so the attribution is the real regression, not background noise. Without one, mark the finding as the strongest hypothesis the artifact supports, not a confirmed cause. **Completion check.** The finding includes the paired diff and regression conclusion, or explicitly says that no paired capture exists and labels the finding as unconfirmed.
6. Hand back a cited diagnosis. Include no fix unless asked. Route to Bug fix or Perf issue once the cause is known. Keep the throughput checkpoint to one line as `throughput checkpoint: n/a, read-only forensics`. **Completion check.** The diagnosis is cited, the fix boundary is honored, the route is named when a cause is known, and the exact throughput checkpoint appears.

**Reply.** Include the artifact and format, the reduced finding, the source location, the artifact paths, and whether a paired capture confirmed it.
